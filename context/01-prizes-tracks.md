## Prize Pool Summary

The Mezo blog post ("The Mezo Hackathon Is Back") states:

> "This year's hackathon features three tracks, **$30,000 worth of veMEZO in prizes**, and additional incentives for teams that continue building after the program ends."

Per-track breakdown (each track has identical structure):
- 1st Place: **$5,000 in veMEZO** + $5,000 in veMEZO grants (milestone-tied)
- 2nd Place: **$3,000 in veMEZO** + $3,000 in veMEZO grants
- Community Choice: **$2,000 in veMEZO** + $2,000 in veMEZO grants

**Prize-only total per track:** $10,000  
**Grant total per track:** $10,000  
**Per track combined:** $20,000  

**Across 3 tracks (prizes only):** $30,000 [CONFIRMED]  
**Across 3 tracks (prizes + grants):** $60,000 in veMEZO total [CONFIRMED from blog]

> Note: Web3voyager says "$30,000 in veMEZO prizes" which matches the prize-only number. Some aggregators cite this as the total — the additional $30K in milestone grants is separate.

**Prize currency:** veMEZO (vote-escrowed MEZO tokens — NOT MUSD as in 2025 hackathon)  
**KYB required:** Yes, for all winning teams

---

## Track 1: Bitcoin Track

**Focus:** Bitcoin-native financial products  
**Target audience:** Traders, DeFi users, institutions  

**What to build:**
- Yield strategies using BTC collateral
- Lending infrastructure
- Payment tools
- Treasury management tools
- Any Bitcoin-native financial product that uses MUSD as the stablecoin layer

**Integration requirements:**
- Must integrate MUSD
- Must integrate MEZO
- Working testnet deployment required

---

## Track 2: MUSD Track

**Focus:** Consumer experiences powered by Bitcoin-backed MUSD  
**Target audience:** Everyday users, consumer apps, commerce builders  

**What to build:**
- Payments using MUSD
- E-commerce or creator monetization using MUSD
- Gaming integrations
- Any consumer-facing app where MUSD is the payment/savings asset

**Integration requirements:**
- Must integrate MUSD
- Must integrate MEZO
- Working testnet deployment required

---

## Track 3: MEZO Track

**Focus:** Making MEZO token more productive  
**Target audience:** Protocol/infra builders, governance tooling  

**What to build:**
- Staking products/dashboards
- Governance tools (veBTC voting, veMEZO voting, gauge management)
- Liquidity infrastructure (LP tooling, gauge optimization)
- New MEZO token utility models
- Infrastructure (keeper automation, liquidation bots, analytics)

**Integration requirements:**
- Must integrate MUSD
- Must integrate MEZO
- Working testnet deployment required
